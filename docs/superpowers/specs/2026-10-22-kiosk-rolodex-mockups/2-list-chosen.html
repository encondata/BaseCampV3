The Rolodex list

Here is what I understood, so you can correct it. Lines marked my guess are mine, not yours.

Who sees it: a Rolodex tile on Home for anyone signed in as admin or higher (admin, super admin, developer). Staff, workers and move-password sign-ins never see it.
Where: the iOS and Android apps, on phones and tablets.
What it lists (my guess): every worker you can see in the portal, not just the current move's crew. Archived workers stay hidden.
Order (my guess): A–Z by first name, with letter sections and the jump strip. Each row shows the photo or gray initials, the name, job title and company, and a status chip.
Finding someone: search by name, job title or company, plus All, Active, Standby and Blacklist filters.
Tablet: the list on the left and the selected worker's page on the right. A phone opens the worker page full screen.
Connection (my guess): the list loads live from the server. With no connection it shows the last list it loaded, read-only, and saving waits until you're back online.

Chosen: B · A–Z list

This works like the phone's Contacts app: alphabetical sections and an A–Z strip to jump with. It fits more people on screen and works well with many workers who have no photo yet. On a tablet the worker page opens beside the list.
