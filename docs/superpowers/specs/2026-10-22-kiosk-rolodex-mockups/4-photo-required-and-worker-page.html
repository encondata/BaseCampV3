The worker page

Here is what I understood, so you can correct it. Lines marked my guess are mine, not yours.

Opening it: tap a row. On a tablet it fills the right side; on a phone it opens full screen.
Top: the photo with a camera badge, name, job title and company, a status chip and an RFID enrolled chip.
New photo: tap the photo for Take new photo (back camera) or Choose photo, then position it. My guess: since photos are required, there is no Remove photo.
Edit: first and last name, preferred name, job title, phone, email, company, trade, level and status. Blacklist needs a note and can't be applied to yourself, the same as the portal.
Read-only (my guess): employee ID and RFID tag. RFID stays with RFID Enroll.
Not shown (my guess): home address, certifications and notes, which stay in the portal.
Limits (my guess): if a worker also has a portal login ranked above yours, the server refuses the edit and the app says why.
